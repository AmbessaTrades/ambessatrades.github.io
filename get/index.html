AfriFluent.

African languages, starting with Kiswahili (Kenya).

Download for Android

Version 0.3.0. No account, no ads.

What’s new

  • AfriFluent is an Android app now, with its own icon.
  • Moving over? Save a backup where you used it before, then choose that file in the app. Your teacher link comes along.
  • Listen keeps playing with the phone locked. Loop repeats the day’s sentences until you stop.
  • Soak for an hour or an hour and a half, hands free.
  • The Phrasebook is a map of places. A place takes colour as you say its lines.
  • Your teacher hears your lines on her own phone and sends her voice back.
  • Today waits for you: a slow start or an “um” never cuts you off.
If your phone asks
  1. Chrome may warn about the file: tap Download anyway.
  2. Tap Open, then Install.
  3. If installs from Chrome are not allowed yet: tap Settings, turn on Allow from this source, go back, then tap Install.
  4. If Play Protect asks: tap Install anyway.
